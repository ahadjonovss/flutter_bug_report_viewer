flutter_bug_report · viewer
Nothing leaves your machine — connect-src 'none'

Drop a bundle — .zip, .json or .txt

Or paste the log. Or pick the file.

A bundle is what flutter_bug_report attaches to a bug report: the app's log, redacted and bounded, with the report's own description and a screenshot beside it.

It is read here in the page and never uploaded — there is no server to upload it to. The bundle was redacted on the device before it was written, but it still carries real data, and a screenshot.png cannot be redacted at all. Save this page and it works with no network at all.
